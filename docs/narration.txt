Every month, a small business has to close its books, and the hardest line is payroll. The bank statement shows what left the account — but that single number hides the truth. Once you add gross pay and employer social-security contributions, the real cost of employing your team is far higher than the transfer suggests. Archon finds that hidden cost automatically.

A single payroll event leaves three documents, and each tells only part of the story. The bank confirmation shows the net amount that was transferred. The payroll register holds the full employer cost. The payslips break it down, person by person. Archon's Event Linker agent fuses all three into one accurate financial event. In the sample report, the bank transfers about fourteen thousand euros, while the true employer cost is over eighteen thousand. That twenty-eight percent gap is the employer social-security wedge alone — and once you add the employee contributions and tax withheld, the full understatement is larger still. None of it is visible until the three documents are correlated.

Archon runs entirely on Nebius Serverless AI. Two on-demand CPU jobs do the heavy lifting — one extracts every document, one runs the financial analysis — and both shut themselves down when they finish. A CPU AI endpoint hosts the FastAPI backend that orchestrates them. The frontier models live in the Nebius Inference API: Qwen two-point-five Vision, with seventy-two billion parameters, reads scanned and Greek-language documents; and Llama three-point-three, seventy billion, writes the executive summary. Object Storage and managed PostgreSQL hold the raw files and the structured results. There is no always-on GPU — the containers stay cheap CPU instances.

We don't just claim accuracy — we measure it. Archon ships an evaluation harness that scores the real pipeline agents against a labelled corpus of forty Greek payroll cases. Under perfect extraction, classification, field, and fusion accuracy all reach one hundred percent — the employer cost is reported to the cent, every time. And the harness earns its place: it surfaced a real defect. Two of the four validation rules never fire, because the fields they depend on are never extracted. That is a number, not a hunch. Measured against the naive bank-only baseline, Archon recovers more than one hundred thirty-three thousand euros of understated payroll across the corpus — roughly seventy percent over the bank figure.
